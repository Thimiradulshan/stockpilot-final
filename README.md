# StockPilot

StockPilot is a Sales, Inventory & Business Management System developed using Laravel.

## Overview

StockPilot helps a business manage products, categories, suppliers, customers, purchases, sales, invoices, payments, stock movements and reports from a centralized web application.

## Main Features

- Secure user authentication
- Role-based authorization
- Dashboard
- Category management
- Supplier management
- Product management
- Customer management
- Purchase management
- Stock management
- Sales / POS
- Invoice management
- Payment management
- Reports
- Stock Ledger
- Invoice printing
- Report printing
- Stock Ledger printing
- Light / Dark theme

## User Roles

### Administrator
Full system access.

### Sales User
Access to sales-related functionality including customers, invoices, payments and sales reports.

### Stock User
Access to products, categories, suppliers, purchases and stock-related functionality.
